I am a Faculty of Engineering student specializing in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Moratuwa and an alumnus of Ananda College. I enjoy building innovative solutions, exploring emerging technologies, and continually growing as a developer and problem-solver. I am passionate about artificial intelligence and its potential to solve real-world problems.

**Final year project · NeuralWave: SAR super-resolution with GPU acceleration**
